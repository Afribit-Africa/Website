# Afribit Africa

**Empowering Communities Through Bitcoin Adoption in Africa**

---

## About Afribit Africa

Afribit Africa is a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering Bitcoin adoption and financial inclusion across African communities, with a primary focus on Kenya's Kibera settlement. We bridge the gap between traditional communities and the global Bitcoin economy through education, merchant onboarding, and community development programs.

## Our Mission

To empower African communities by providing access to Bitcoin technology, creating economic opportunities, and building sustainable infrastructure for the future of digital finance.
